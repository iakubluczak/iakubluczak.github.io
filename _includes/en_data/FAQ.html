Frequently Asked Questions

What will the weather be like?

In early October in Poznań the average temperature is around 12–15°C during the day. We recommend layered clothing and bringing something warmer for the evening.

What is the dress code?

Smart and elegant. Gentlemen usually wear suits (black is fine, preferably with a non-white shirt), and ladies typically choose dresses in various colours. Please avoid all-white or all-black outfits.

Will transportation be arranged?

The survey includes a question about transport. Let us know your needs and we’ll do our best to help arrange travel.
